About the festival

From 1,000 fans in 2025 to two days in 2026

After a blowout debut in Skovdalen last year, where metal fans poured in from across the country and Scandinavia, Port of Hell is expanding to two days. Created by ARM and Marienhaab Koncert- og Kulturforening, with backing from Aalborg Kommune and Aalborg Events.

Rock and metal festival in North Jutland

Rock and metal festival in Aalborg — for all of North Jutland

Port of Hell is a rock and metal festival in Aalborg that brings together fans from across North Jutland and the rest of Denmark for two days of growls, riffs and deafening energy in Skovdalen.

As one of the few major rock and metal festivals in North Jutland, Port of Hell has quickly put itself on the map as one of Denmark's best rock festivals for people who want to experience hard music up close to the stage and close to each other. Whether you're coming from Aalborg, the rest of North Jutland, or travelling far to experience a genuine rock festival in Denmark, Port of Hell offers a line-up, an atmosphere and a sense of community that's hard to find anywhere else. Tickets for the rock and metal festival are sold online via Billetto, where you can see the current ticket types and prices.
