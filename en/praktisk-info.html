Ready for hell?

Packing List

What to remember

  • Cash or MobilePay for food, drinks and merch
  • Rain gear — Skovdalen is outdoors, and the weather in September can change fast
  • Earplugs, if you're sensitive to loud sound
  • Comfortable footwear — you'll be on your feet most of the day
  • Valid ticket (printed or on your phone) and ID
Not Allowed

What not to bring

  • Glass and can packaging
  • Your own food and drinks (the food stalls on site have you covered)
  • Weapons or other dangerous items
  • Drones or other airborne recording equipment without prior agreement
Getting there

Transport & parking

Public TransportSkovdalen is centrally located in Aalborg — check Rejseplanen for the bus routes closest to the festival grounds.
ParkingLimited parking in the area — we encourage walking, cycling or taking the bus if possible.
AccommodationSee our camping and hotel recommendations on the About page.
Accessibility

Everyone should be able to join in

We want as many people as possible to be able to experience Port of Hell. If you have a disability or specific needs — for example wheelchair access, a companion ticket, or anything else that could make your visit easier — please write to us in advance at portofhell@portofhell.dk.
